Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Who we are

HAYCHIC Boutique is a small boutique based in Abilene, Texas. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and protect information you share when shopping with us or using our website at haychicboutique.com.

Information we collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Email address — when you sign up for our newsletter or submit a form.
  • Name and contact details — when you place an order or request an item.
  • Order information — items purchased, amounts paid, and order status.
  • Payment information — processed securely by Stripe. We never store your card details.
  • Usage data — basic analytics about how you navigate the site (pages visited, time on site). This data is anonymous.

How we use your information

  • To process and fulfill your orders.
  • To communicate order updates, shipping info, and support responses.
  • To send marketing emails if you have opted in (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • To improve our website and shopping experience.

Third-party services

We use the following third-party services that may process your data:

  • Stripe — secure payment processing. Stripe Privacy Policy
  • Firebase (Google) — our database and order management system. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Fonts — fonts loaded from Google servers.
  • Shippo — shipping label generation for orders.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to any third party.

Cookies

Our site uses cookies to function properly and improve your experience. See our Cookie Policy for details.

Data retention

We keep your information for as long as needed to provide our services and comply with applicable law. You can request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of any personal information we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us via the Support page.

Childrens privacy

Our site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of the State of Texas, United States.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about your privacy? Reach us via the Support page.
