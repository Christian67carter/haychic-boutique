How HAYCHIC pre-orders work

Some HAYCHIC items are ordered especially for you and may not be on hand when you purchase.

  • Typical turnaround: about 2–3 weeks before your item reaches HAYCHIC.
  • Sometimes faster: certain items may arrive within about a week, but this is not guaranteed.
  • Clear labels: each product should say IN STOCK or PRE-ORDER before checkout.
  • Quality check: you’ll be contacted before final delivery or shipment when needed.

Frequently asked questions

What does pre-order mean?

A pre-order item is not currently on hand. It is ordered after purchase and usually takes around 2–3 weeks to arrive to HAYCHIC before final delivery, pickup or shipment.

Can I request an item that isn’t listed?

Yes. Use the Request an Item form and send details or a reference photo/link.

How do I know if something is in stock?

Products are labeled IN STOCK or PRE-ORDER directly on the product card and product page.

Do you offer pickup or delivery?

Local pickup or delivery can be offered when available. Final options should be shown at checkout.
