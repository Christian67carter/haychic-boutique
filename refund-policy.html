Refund Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Overview

At HAYCHIC, every piece is carefully inspected before it ships. Because we are a small boutique, all refund requests are reviewed on a case-by-case basis and are not guaranteed. Please read this policy carefully before placing your order.

You have 3 days from the date your order is marked delivered to submit a refund request. Requests received after this window will not be considered.

Eligibility Window

Your 3-day window begins the moment your tracking shows Delivered — not the date you open the package. We recommend checking your mail promptly after delivery so you have the full window to evaluate your order.

Requests submitted on Day 4 or later will not be reviewed, regardless of the reason.

How to Submit a Request

To submit a refund request, email us at hayden@haychicboutique.com within 3 days of delivery and include:

  • Your order number
  • Your full name and the email used to place the order
  • A brief description of the issue
  • Clear photos of the item(s) in question

Incomplete requests (missing photos, order number, etc.) will not be processed.

Review & Approval

Submitting a refund request does not guarantee a refund. Each request is reviewed individually. We reserve the right to approve or decline any request at our sole discretion.

Requests are more likely to be approved for:

  • Items that arrive damaged or defective
  • Items that are significantly different from what was described
  • Incorrect items shipped due to our error

Requests are generally not approved for:

  • Change of mind or buyer's remorse
  • Sizing issues (please review sizing carefully before ordering)
  • Minor color variations due to screen display differences
  • Items returned without prior approval

If Your Request Is Approved

We will contact you with return instructions. Items must be sent back unworn, unwashed, with original tags attached, within 5 days of approval. Return shipping costs are the customer's responsibility unless the return is due to our error.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method once the item is received and inspected. Please allow 5–10 business days for the refund to appear on your statement.

Non-Returnable Items

  • Final sale items
  • Accessories (jewelry, hair accessories, etc.)
  • Items that have been worn, washed, or altered
  • Items without original tags

Exchanges

We do not offer direct exchanges at this time. If your refund request is approved, you are welcome to place a new order for a different size or style.

Lost or Stolen Packages

Once a carrier marks a package as delivered, HAYCHIC is not responsible for lost or stolen packages. Please contact your carrier directly to file a claim. We are happy to provide your tracking information to assist.

Questions about your order?

Reach out and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.
