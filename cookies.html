Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and understand how you use the site.

How we use cookies

HAYCHIC Boutique uses cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential cookies — required for the site to function. These include session cookies used by Firebase Authentication to keep you logged in and shopping cart cookies to maintain your session. These cannot be disabled without breaking site functionality.
  • Preference cookies — remember your choices, such as whether you have accepted our cookie notice.
  • Analytics cookies — anonymous data about how visitors use our site (pages visited, time spent, referral source). This helps us improve the shopping experience. No personally identifiable information is collected.

Third-party cookies

Some cookies are set by third-party services we use:

  • Firebase / Google — authentication and database services may set session cookies.
  • Stripe — payment processing may set cookies for fraud prevention and security.
  • Google Fonts — fonts loaded from Google servers may involve data transferred to Google.

Managing cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies. Note that disabling essential cookies may prevent parts of the site from working correctly.

Links to cookie settings for common browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Your consent

When you first visit our site, we display a cookie notice. By continuing to browse and clicking Accept, you consent to our use of non-essential cookies. You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing your cookies in your browser settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy as our practices change. The date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
