Refund Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Our approach

At HAYCHIC Boutique, we handle refund and return requests on a case-by-case basis. We care about your experience and want to make things right — just reach out and we will work with you.

How to request a refund

Contact us through the Support page within a reasonable time of receiving your order. Please include your order number and a brief description of the issue. We will review your request and respond as quickly as possible.

What we consider

When reviewing a refund or return request, we take into account factors such as the condition of the item, the reason for the return, and the time since purchase. We will do our best to find a fair resolution — whether that is a refund, exchange, or store credit.

Pre-orders

If a pre-order item becomes unavailable or cannot be fulfilled, we will issue a full refund automatically. If you need to cancel a pre-order before it ships, contact us and we will do our best to accommodate you.

Items we cannot accept back

For hygiene and safety reasons, we are unable to accept returns on items that appear to have been worn, washed, or damaged after delivery. Final sale items are not eligible for return.

Damaged or incorrect items

If your order arrives damaged or you receive the wrong item, contact us right away — we will make it right at no cost to you.

Refund processing

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. Processing time may vary depending on your bank or card issuer, but typically takes 5–10 business days to appear.

Contact

Have a question about an order? Reach us via the Support page. We are a small team and we appreciate your patience — we will get back to you as soon as we can.
